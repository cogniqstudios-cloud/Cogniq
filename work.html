Case Studies

Real-world business problems broken down into AI-driven solutions. Each example shows how operational inefficiencies can be removed using simple systems.

Local Service Business — Enquiry Bottleneck

Problem

Customers were sending enquiries via email, Instagram, and website forms — replies were delayed by hours.

Insight

The business had no central system for handling incoming requests.

Solution

AI-assisted inbox triage + automated response templates + structured booking flow.

Outcome

Estimated response time reduced from hours to minutes.

Café — Manual Order Handling

Problem

Staff were spending time answering repetitive questions about menu items and availability.

Insight

Most customer questions were predictable and repeatable.

Solution

AI chatbot trained on menu + automated FAQ system integrated into website.

Outcome

Reduced staff interruptions and improved customer experience flow.

Small Business Website — Conversion Issue

Problem

Website traffic existed but visitors were not making enquiries.

Insight

The website lacked clarity, structure, and action-driven messaging.

Solution

Redesigned homepage structure with clear value proposition + AI call-to-action flow.

Outcome

Improved clarity and expected increase in enquiry rate.
